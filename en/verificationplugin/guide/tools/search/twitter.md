## General information

**X. Search** (formerly Twitter Advanced Search) provides a structured interface for building and executing advanced search queries on the X (formerly Twitter) platform. It allows verification professionals to find tweets by keyword, account, date range, language, and engagement metrics without needing to write manual search syntax.

## How to use it

Use the form fields to specify your search criteria:
- Keywords or phrases
- Specific accounts (from or mentioning)
- Date range
- Language
- Minimum engagement thresholds (retweets, likes, replies)

The tool constructs the corresponding X advanced search query and opens the results directly on the platform.

## Use cases

- Finding the first occurrence of a claim or image on X
- Tracking the spread of a piece of content over time
- Searching for eyewitness accounts from a specific location and time period
- Identifying coordinated sharing behaviour

## Tips

- Use exact phrase search (quotes) to find specific claims verbatim
- Combine date ranges with geographic filters to narrow results around a specific event
