## General information

**Video Rights Management** assesses platform licensing guidelines and distribution access parameters for online video URLs. It helps newsrooms quickly evaluate the legal parameters and ownership chains of user-generated videos during active breaking news cycles.

## How to use it

Provide the URL of a video hosted on a supported platform. The tool will retrieve and display the available rights and licensing information associated with that video.

## Use cases

- Determining whether a user-generated video can be republished or broadcast
- Identifying the copyright holder or originating account before licensing negotiations
- Quickly checking rights status on multiple videos during a fast-moving news event

## Important notes

- Rights information is retrieved from platform-provided metadata and may not reflect the full legal picture. Always confirm with your organisation's legal team before publishing third-party content.
- Platform licensing terms can change. Verify current terms of service for the platform in question.
