## General information

**Video Analysis** automates contextual structured scraping on video hosting platforms such as YouTube and Facebook. It fetches deep source data including exact upload times across time zones, channel verification tags, and historical engagement anomalies. It also extracts comment structures to map source links shared by viewers or spot coordinated bot responses.

## How to use it

You can analyse a video by providing:
- a direct URL to a video on a supported platform (YouTube, Facebook, and others)

## How it works

The tool queries platform APIs and public metadata endpoints to retrieve structured data about the video: upload timestamp, channel details, engagement history, and comment data. This allows analysts to establish a timeline of the content and identify unusual distribution patterns.

## Use cases

- Verifying the original upload date and source of a video
- Checking channel credibility and publication history
- Identifying links shared in comments that may indicate coordinated behaviour
- Detecting engagement anomalies that suggest artificial amplification

## Important notes

- Availability of data depends on the platform's public API access and the video's privacy settings.
- Results reflect the state of the platform at the time of the query and may change if the content is edited or removed.
