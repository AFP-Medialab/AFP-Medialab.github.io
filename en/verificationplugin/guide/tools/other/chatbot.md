## General information

**Chatbot** provides an AI-powered conversational assistant. It can answer questions about verification methodology, help guide the use of available tools, and provide contextual assistance during an investigation. The model is hosted in an LM studio locally that the user has to configure on his own to be able to use it. More details see https://lmstudio.ai/docs/app/api.

## How to use it
Download LM studio and a LLM you want to use in the chatbot. Lauch it on a local server.
Then open the Chatbot and chose the model and the prompt you want to run.

## Use cases

- Getting guidance on a factcheck or rhetorical analysis of an article

## Important notes

- The chatbot is an AI assistant and may occasionally provide incorrect or outdated information. Always verify critical information through primary sources.
