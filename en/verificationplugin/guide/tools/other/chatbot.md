## General information

**Chatbot** provides an AI-powered conversational assistant integrated into the Verification Plugin. It can answer questions about verification methodology, help guide the use of available tools, and provide contextual assistance during an investigation.

## How to use it

Open the Chatbot from the navigation bar. Type your question or describe the verification task you are working on. The assistant will respond with guidance, tool suggestions, or relevant information.

## Use cases

- Getting guidance on which tool to use for a specific verification task
- Asking questions about verification best practices
- Receiving step-by-step assistance during a fact-checking workflow

## Important notes

- The chatbot is an AI assistant and may occasionally provide incorrect or outdated information. Always verify critical information through primary sources.
- Do not share sensitive personal data or confidential source information with the chatbot.
