## General information

**XNetwork** is a X search engine that analyse a precise twitter search. You can choose the time window, the content, the lanaguage, etc. You then retrieve lot of metrics : number of tweets, graph of time propagation of the tweets, users liked and retweeted, word cloud about the content of the tweets, etc.

## How to use it

Configure your search and then go throught the result to identify the metrics you are interested in. 

## Use cases

- Evaluate the virality of some content (where it has spread, how much is it shared, etc.)
